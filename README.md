# Skip

A lightweight Chrome extension that improves YouTube video quality with one simple toggle.

Skip is built to stay simple — turn it on, watch your video, and compare the difference instantly.

## Features

- One-click video enhancement
- Before & After comparison slider
- Original vs Enhanced preview
- Motion Blur toggle
- Adjustable Motion Blur strength
- Automatically works on YouTube
- Saves your settings
- Lightweight and simple UI
- No accounts
- No ads
- No tracking

## Preview

Skip includes a live comparison mode that lets you drag a divider across the video and instantly see the difference between the original and enhanced version.
